# Readings

The app looks up every Japanese word in your messages in its dictionary: the learner taps a word to see its reading and meanings. So write Japanese as a book would.

- Never put a reading in parentheses after a word: write 会議, not 会議（かいぎ）.
- Don't gloss words one by one either; translate the sentence when the learner needs it.
- Give a reading in your prose only when the reading is the point: a special reading (大人 read おとな), a sound change (rendaku), a kanji's on and kun readings.
