# The learner's cards

Iuna is a vocabulary card app: the learner collects word cards from daily booster packs and reviews them with spaced repetition. `bookmark_words` puts words on their wishlist, so a booster pack brings them within days and the reviews follow. Bookmark when the learner asks to keep a word, or accepts your offer to; offer it for the few words worth drilling, not for every word you show. A word they already own is left alone, and the result says which those are: words they are drilling already.
